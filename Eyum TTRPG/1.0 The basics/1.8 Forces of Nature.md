## The Four Forces
Nature, Corruption, Blight, and Infection are four affinities that physically spread across the surface of Eyum. Nature is the default state of the world, the land as it was created. The other three are not originally from Isthmus. They are invading planes of existence layered over the physical world, slowly bleeding through and converting whatever they touch (see [[1.7 Plains of Existence]] for their home planes). Each force converts ground, water, creatures, and materials into a new corrupted form. Nature covers most of the world and grows back into corrupted territory slowly, while the three corrupted forces grow into Nature from the edges and from any wound in the land, a forest fire, a war, a collapsed city. Neither side permanently wins.
### Corruption
Corruption is dark purple crystal and stone. It is less a disease or rot and more just a material: Corrupt Stone, Corrupt Metal, Corrupt Crystal, Corrupt Liquid. The ground physically changes from whatever it was into a purple crystalline version of itself. Dirt becomes jagged purple crystal gravel, stone becomes deep violet stone with sharp angular fractures, all ore and metal becomes Corrupt Metal (dark purple-grey streaked with glowing violet veins), and water becomes Corrupt Liquid (similar to water but deep violet, slightly thicker, faintly acidic, tingling on contact). The transformation is total and uniform. Everything in a corrupted zone eventually becomes the same purple crystal. Trees become crystal trees, frozen in place, purple and angular, their leaves replaced with thin violet shards that chime faintly in the wind. Corruption does not affect the air, the air in corrupted territory is normal.

**Spreading onto Tiles:** Each turn (6 seconds), every corrupted 5x5x5 ft cube makes one attack against one randomly selected adjacent non-corrupted cube. The target cube does not matter, stone, dirt, water, air, all are valid. Roll 1d20. On a 15 or higher the target cube becomes corrupted. If the target cube is Nature, the DC is 18. Corruption cannot spread onto Blight or Infection tiles. A cube that fails to spread simply tries again next turn. Corrupted creatures count as a corrupted cube for spreading purposes and automatically corrupt all cubes they occupy and all adjacent cubes at the end of each of their turns.

**Spreading onto Creatures:** Any creature in contact with corrupted material must make a CON save at the end of each turn it remains in contact. The DC is 12 + the number of corrupted cubes adjacent to the creature (standing in a single corrupted cube gives DC 12, being fully surrounded gives DC 20). On a failure the creature gains 1 stack of Corrupt.

**Corrupt Condition:** The Corrupt condition already exists in [[2.4 Conditions]] with its current effects (deals 1d4 Corruption damage per stack per turn directly to Health). In addition to that existing damage, the following physical effects apply at higher stack counts. Each stack still deals its 1d4 damage per turn independently.

- 1 stack: As per the existing condition. Eyes develop a faint violet tint.
- 3 stacks: -2 to all d20 rolls, -5 ft movement speed. Purple veins become visible under skin. Deals 3d4 Corruption damage per turn.
- 5 stacks: -3 to all d20 rolls, -10 ft movement speed. Skin hardens and takes on a purple-grey color, fingertips crystallize. Deals 5d4 Corruption damage per turn.
- 7 stacks: -4 to all d20 rolls, -15 ft movement speed. Limbs become stiff, crystal growths appear on joints, voice becomes flat and monotone. Deals 7d4 Corruption damage per turn.
- 10 stacks: The creature dies and its body petrifies into a solid purple crystal statue over 1 minute, or if it has the Corrupted monster modifier it gains that modifier instantly instead of petrifying. Creatures with the modifier still petrify when killed. The statue is Corrupt Stone and counts as a corrupted cube.

Stacks fade naturally at a rate of 1 stack per hour once the creature leaves corrupted territory. A Remove Curse spell removes 2 stacks per upcast level.

**Corrupted Modifier:** Creatures that reach 10 stacks of Corrupt and possess the Corrupted monster modifier (see [[7.3.5 Monster Modifiers]]) are converted instantly. They do not speak, eat, sleep, or breathe, and exist only to spread the corruption by touching everything they can reach. They are immune to gaining further Corrupt stacks.

**Materials:** Corrupted territory converts everything into usable materials. These are listed in full with all crafting stats and alchemical uses in [[2.6 Materials]] and [[6.3 Alchemy]]. The four material types are Corrupt Stone (from any stone or dirt), Corrupt Metal (from metal ores), Corrupt Crystal (from gem deposits), and Corrupt Liquid (from any liquid). All four deal 1d4 Corruption damage on skin contact and force a DC 12 CON save per minute of contact.
### Blight
Blight is yellow-brown rot, the process of decay weaponized and made permanent, and it hangs in the air as a constant yellow-brown haze of drifting spores. Blighted ground is spongy and soft, releasing a puff of yellow spores when you step on it. Trees become hollow shells with bark peeling off in long yellowed strips while fungal growths cover every surface--shelf fungi stacked up trunks, puffballs in the dirt, thin yellow mycelium threads under the soil. Pools of stagnant yellow-brown water bubble in low spots, and the air is thick with drifting spores, smelling of mold, wet rot, and something sweet like overripe fruit.

Blight destroys rather than replacing. A blighted iron vein is not Blight Iron, it is rusted, brittle, worthless ore that crumbles when mined. Blighted wood is spongy and unusable. Blighted water is undrinkable and carries disease. Nothing harvested from blighted ground has material value, the only usable things come from the spores and fungal growths on the creatures that live there.

**Spreading onto Tiles:** Each turn, every blighted cube makes one attack against one randomly selected adjacent cube. Blight can only target Nature cubes or cubes containing organic material, it cannot spread onto Corruption or Infection, nor onto bare stone, metal, or inorganic material. Roll 1d20 with the base DC at 13. For each additional blighted cube adjacent to the target beyond the first, add +1 to the roll. If the target is completely surrounded by blighted cubes, add an additional +5. On a success the target becomes blighted.

**Spreading onto Creatures:** The blight makes a contested spread roll: 1d20 + bonuses from adjacent blighted cubes versus the creature's CON save. If blight wins, the creature gains 1 stack of Blighted. Blighted creatures with the Blighted monster modifier automatically spread blight to all Nature cubes and organic cubes they occupy and all adjacent such cubes at the end of each of their turns.

**Blighted Condition:** Needs to be added to [[2.4 Conditions]].

- 1 stack: -1 CON. Coughs up yellow spores once per turn as a free action (no mechanical effect).
- 3 stacks: -2 CON, -5 ft movement speed. Yellow fungal growths visible on skin.
- 5 stacks: -3 CON, -10 ft movement speed. Open wounds weep yellow fluid instead of blood, fungal shelves grow from joints and orifices.
- 7 stacks: -4 CON, -15 ft movement speed. Body mostly consumed by fungus, cannot speak, only wheezes spores.
- 10 stacks: The creature dies and its body becomes a Blighted Spore Hive (stationary object, AC 10, 50 HP, releases 10 ft radius spore cloud dealing 2d6 Blight damage per turn to creatures inside until destroyed). The original creature cannot be resurrected, its body is gone, or if it has the Blighted monster modifier it gains that modifier instantly instead of becoming a hive. Creatures with the modifier still become a hive when killed.

Stacks do not fade naturally. Remove Curse removes 2 stacks per upcast level. A DC 15 Medicine check with 1 hour of care removes 1 stack.

**Blighted Modifier:** Creatures that reach 10 stacks and have the Blighted monster modifier (see [[7.3.5 Monster Modifiers]]) are converted instead of dying. They are immune to further Blighted stacks and spread blight automatically.

**Materials:** Blight produces only organic harvestables (Blight Spores, Rotting Wood, Fungal Shelf). These are listed with full stats in [[2.6 Materials]].

### Infection

Infection is green sickness that spreads through contact and hangs in the air as constant clouds of green gas around infected territory and creatures. Infected ground is coated in a layer of green slime with boils and pustules that burst randomly, spraying fluid and releasing the gas. The air is thick and humid, smelling chemical like chlorine and rotting meat. The terrain underneath is still there and unchanged, and if the infection is purged the land is perfectly fine.

Infection infects living creatures, possesses them, and uses them to spread further, but it can always be cured and never directly kills its host. Infected creatures are covered in green mucus, weeping sores, and pustules, with extra limbs, eyes, and mouths growing from random parts of their body. The creature is in constant pain and has gone insane from it, attacking everything indiscriminately. The infection makes the host physically stronger, more muscle, more aggression, more resistant to damage, but whatever intelligence was there is completely gone, replaced by a feral drive to spread through violence.

**Spreading onto Tiles:** Each turn, every infected cube makes one attack against one randomly selected adjacent non-infected cube. Infection can target any cube, Nature or otherwise. Roll 1d20 with a base DC of 18. There are no adjacency bonuses from other infected cubes. Each time a cube fails to spread, its DC permanently decreases by 1 for future attempts. On a success the target becomes infected and the new cube starts at DC 18. This means infection spreads very slowly at first but becomes more aggressive over time if left unchecked.

**Spreading onto Creatures:** A creature in contact with an infected cube or an Infected creature must make a CON save at the end of each of its turns. The DC starts at 10. Each time the creature is forced to make this save, whether it passes or fails, the DC permanently increases by 1 for all future saves against infection for that creature. On a failure the creature gains 1 stack of Sickened and 1 stack of Infected. On a success nothing happens but the DC still increases.

**Infected Condition:** Needs to be added to [[2.4 Conditions]].

- 1 stack: -1 to all mental stats (INT, WIS, CHA). Green mucus seeps from pores.
- 3 stacks: -2 to mental stats. Boils form on skin. Disadvantage on CHA checks.
- 5 stacks: -3 to mental stats, all mental stats halved (rounded down, applied after flat penalty). Extra limbs begin growing from torso. Creature attacks nearest target each turn if no target is specified.
- 7 stacks: Mental stats reduced to 2. Body fully covered in mucus, boils, and extra limbs. Creature is under GM control and attacks anything non-Infected.
- 10 stacks: The creature becomes fully Infected. Apply the Infected monster modifier permanently. The original personality is gone and cannot be restored, but the creature is still technically alive.

Stacks can be removed with Remove Curse (2 stacks per upcast level) or any spell or item that cures disease. Stacks do not fade naturally, but they never directly kill the host. A creature with 10 stacks is not dead, just permanently changed.

**Infected Modifier:** Creatures that reach 10 stacks gain the Infected monster modifier permanently (see [[7.3.5 Monster Modifiers]]). They are immune to further Infected stacks.

**Materials:** Infection produces only organic harvestables (Infected Flesh, Mucus Glands, Pustule Clusters). These are listed with full stats and alchemical uses in [[2.6 Materials]] and [[6.3 Alchemy]].

### Nature

Nature is the default state of the world. It does not have a spreading mechanic the way the other three do--it simply is whatever the land would be without corruption. Forests, grasslands, jungles, wetlands. It grows back into corrupted territory slowly: 5% per adjacent Nature cube per month for any corrupted cube. This is too slow to win on its own but just fast enough that the corrupted territories cannot overrun everything. Left alone, the balance holds.

Nature has no condition. You do not gain stacks of Nature by standing in a forest. It is the baseline. Nature's mechanical identity is that it is what the other three are trying to destroy and what you are trying to protect.

### Manual Purification

At combat scale, manually clearing corrupted cubes works as follows:

- Corruption: Pulling up crystal growths and vines takes 10 minutes per 5 ft cube.
- Blight: Excavating contaminated soil and replacing it with clean fill takes 1 day of labor per 5 ft cube.
- Infection: Burning off the slime layer with fire takes 10 minutes per 5 ft cube. The terrain underneath is undamaged.
