## The Four Forces
Nature, Corruption, Blight, and Infection are four affinities that physically spread across the surface of Eyum. Nature is the default state of the world, the land as it was created. The other three are not originally from Isthmus at all. They are invading planes of existence layered over the physical world, slowly bleeding through and converting whatever they touch (see [[1.7 Plains of Existence]] for their home planes). Each force converts ground, water, creatures, and materials into a new corrupted form. Nature covers most of the world and grows back into corrupted territory slowly, while the three corrupted forces grow into Nature anywhere they can. The forces typically attack Eyum, the domain of Darwin directly but sometimes venture out into the other plains. 
### Corruption
Corruption is dark purple crystal and stone. It is less a disease or rot and more just a material: Corrupt Stone, Corrupt Metal, Corrupt Crystal, Corrupt Liquid. The ground physically changes from whatever it was into a purple crystalline version of itself. Dirt becomes jagged purple crystal gravel, stone becomes deep violet stone with sharp angular fractures, all ore and metal becomes Corrupt Metal (dark purple-grey streaked with glowing violet veins), and water becomes Corrupt Liquid (similar to water but deep violet, slightly thicker, faintly acidic, tingling on contact). The transformation is total and uniform. Everything in a corrupted zone eventually becomes the same purple crystal. Trees become crystal trees, frozen in place, purple and angular, their leaves replaced with thin violet shards that chime faintly in the wind. The air in corrupted territory is normal, curruption can only spred through phsyical contact.

**Spreading onto Tiles:** Each turn (6 seconds), every corrupted 5x5x5 ft cube makes one attack against one randomly selected adjacent non-corrupted cube or go dormint if all ajacent tiles are already currupted. The target cube must be made of some physical material, solid or liquid, it does not all have to be made of the same material, but all matieral in that cube will be converted. Roll 1d20. On a 15 or higher the target cube becomes corrupted, it takes 3 turns for a tile to become fully currupted after this and start currupting their own tiles. If the target cube is/contains Nature, the DC is 18. Corruption cannot spread onto Blight or Infection tiles. A cube that fails to spread simply tries again next turn. Corrupted creatures count as a corrupted cube for spreading purposes and automatically corrupt all cubes they occupy and all adjacent cubes at the end of each of their turns with the same checks/dcs, the only difference is that they only currupt the tile they are actually standing on, that tile then will start to currupt the land around it. If a Currupt tile is inert (not attacking) for more than 1 month it will become perminently inert, the materials are still potent and can spred onto cretaures but even if the tiles around it are purified it will no longer spread. 

**Spreading onto Creatures:** Any creature in contact with corrupted material must make a CON save at the end of each turn it remains in contact. The DC is 12 + the number of corrupted cubes adjacent to the creature (standing in a single corrupted cube gives DC 12, being fully surrounded gives DC 20). On a failure the creature gains 1 stack of [[Eyum TTRPG/2.0 Reference Tables/2.4 Conditions#Physical Conditions|Currupt]]. Creatures that reach 10 stacks of Corrupt and die then are given the Corrupted monster modifier (see [[7.3.5 Monster Modifiers]]). They do not speak, eat, sleep, or breathe, and exist only to spread the corruption by touching everything they can reach. 

**Materials:** Corrupted territory converts everything into usable materials. These are listed in full with all crafting stats and alchemical uses in [[2.6.1 Metals and Stones Materials]] and [[6.3 Alchemy]]. The four material types are Corrupt Stone (from any stone or dirt, dirt makes more of a gravel of this material where stone is solid), Corrupt Metal (from metal), Corrupt Crystal (from gem deposits and organic things), and Corrupt Liquid (from any liquid). 
### Blight
Blight is yellow-brown rot, the process of decay weaponized and made permanent, and it hangs in the air as a constant yellow-brown haze of drifting spores. Blighted ground is spongy and soft, releasing a puff of yellow spores when you step on it. Trees become hollow shells with bark peeling off in long yellowed strips while fungal growths cover every surface--shelf fungi stacked up trunks, puffballs in the dirt, thin yellow mycelium threads under the soil. Pools of stagnant yellow-brown water bubble in low spots, and the air is thick with drifting spores, smelling of mold, wet rot, and something sweet like overripe fruit.

Blight destroys rather than replacing. A blighted iron vein is not Blight Iron, it is rusted, brittle, worthless ore that crumbles when mined. Blighted wood is spongy and unusable. Blighted water is undrinkable and carries disease dealing 30 Blight damage to anyhting which drinks it and granting 2 Blighted stacks. Nothing harvested from blighted ground has any real material value but some things can be harvested which are found in [[2.6.3 Special and Other Materials]].

**Spreading onto Tiles:** Each turn, every blighted cube makes one attack against one randomly selected adjacent cube. Blight can only target Nature cubes or cubes containing organic material, it cannot spread onto Corruption or Infection, nor onto bare stone, metal, or inorganic material. Roll 1d20 with the base DC at 13. For each additional blighted cube adjacent to the target beyond the first, add +1 to the roll. If the target is completely surrounded by blighted cubes, add an additional +5. On a success the target becomes blighted over the next 5 turns. After the process is completed it will begin attempting to spread as well. If a Blighted tile makes no attacks for 1 month it begins to die, over the course of the next 3 months it will slowly be reduced in potency until it fades away completly, destroying all organic material in the process and leaving behind nothing but the inorganic material which was originally there with no traces of Blight at all. While in a Blighted tile you must make a CON save (DC 13, additional to the Blight saves) or gain the Suffocating condition from the thick haze for that turn. 

**Spreading onto Creatures:** The blight makes a contested spread roll: 1d20 +1 from each adjacent blighted cube or creature versus the creature's CON save. If blight wins, the creature gains 1 stack of [[Eyum TTRPG/2.0 Reference Tables/2.4 Conditions#Physical Conditions|Blighted]]. Creatures that reach 10 stacks will gain the Blighted monster modifier (see [[7.3.5 Monster Modifiers]]).
### Infection
Infection is green sickness that spreads through contact and hangs in the air as constant clouds of green gas around infected territory and creatures. Infected ground is coated in a layer of green slime with boils and pustules that burst randomly, spraying fluid and releasing the gas. The air is thick and humid, smelling chemical like chlorine and rotting meat. The terrain underneath is still there and unchanged, and if the infection is purged the land is somewhat fine, although all the plants will have died. Infection infects living creatures, possesses them, and uses them to spread further, but it can always be cured and never directly kills its host. Infected creatures are covered in green mucus, weeping sores, and pustules. The creature is in constant pain and has gone insane attacking everything indiscriminately. The infection makes the host physically stronger, more aggression, more resistant to damage, but whatever intelligence was there is completely gone, replaced by a feral drive to spread through violence.

**Spreading onto Tiles:** Each turn, every infected cube makes one attack against one randomly selected adjacent non-infected cube. Infection can target any cube, Nature or otherwise which it will simply coat with a thick slime. Roll 1d20 with a base DC of 18. Each time a cube fails to spread, its DC permanently decreases by 1 for future attempts for that specific tile. On a success the target becomes infected and the new cube is created over the next turn which then begins its spred. The DC lowering from a tile being attacked is not specific to the tile which attacked it, if other tiles attack it first the DC is lower for **all** tiles which are attacking it. This means infection spreads very slowly at first but becomes more aggressive over time if left unchecked. Some of these materials on the tiles can be harvested and found in [[2.6.3 Special and Other Materials]].

**Spreading onto Creatures:** A creature in contact with an infected cube or an Infected creature must make a CON save at the end of each of its turns. The DC starts at 10. Each time the creature is forced to make this save, whether it passes or fails, the DC semi-permanently increases by 1 for all future saves against infection. On a failure the creature gains 1 stack of Sickened and 1 stack of [[Eyum TTRPG/2.0 Reference Tables/2.4 Conditions#Physical Conditions|Infected]]. On a success nothing happens but the DC still increases. If the creature has no contact with infection and has no infected stacks for 1 month, the DC will begin to lower back to 10, lowering by 1 per month. Ceatures and monsters that reach X stacks gain the Infected monster modifier permanently (see [[7.3.5 Monster Modifiers]]), for more information see [[Eyum TTRPG/2.0 Reference Tables/2.4 Conditions#Physical Conditions|Infected]]. 
### Nature
Nature is the default state of the world. It does not have a spreading mechanic the way the other three do, it simply is whatever the land would be without corruption. Forests, grasslands, jungles, wetlands. It grows back into corrupted territory slowly. Nature has no condition, you do not gain stacks of Nature by standing in a forest, it is just the baseline. 
### Manual Purification
Although purification is best done through spells it can also be done manually by clearing corrupted cubes. This works as follows:

- **Corruption**: Completley digging up and removing all currupted tiles and isolating them from material they can grow into then waiting long enough for them to become inert. 
- **Blight**: Excavating contaminated and material and burning it. 
- **Infection**: Excavating contaminated and material and burning it.
