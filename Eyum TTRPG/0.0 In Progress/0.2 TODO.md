# Making a New Affinity
Creating a new affinity touches nearly every system in the handbook. Use this as a checklist whenever adding one. 

**Affinity Table Entry (2.1 Affinities)**
Add the new affinity to the table in [[2.1 Affinities]] with its name, prerequisite affinities and total point investment required to unlock it (the (x) notation), what element it is weak to, and its display color. Determine whether it's a base element (no prerequisites like Fire/Earth/Water/Air), a simple combination (two base elements like Lightning = Fire + Air), a complex combination (three elements like Frostfire), or a high-tier affinity (four or more elements like Storm or Corruption). Follow the existing prerequisite patterns: base elements require nothing, simple combos require 10+10, element + Force combos require 3 Force + 10 element, and top-tier combos scale up to 40-60 total points. For all of the new ones we will work them like quantum where they require complex affinities not just high levels of base affinities. 

**Damage Type (2.2 Damage Types)**
Add the affinity's damage type to [[2.2 Damage Types]]. Every affinity has a corresponding damage type. Define its grouping (Elemental, Magical, Physical, Special), what it is weak to and strong against per the affinity table, and any special damage behavior (bypassing Vitality, applying conditions on hit, etc.). The damage type must match the weakness/resistance chain defined in the affinity table.

**Unique Injuries and Conditions (2.3 Injuries)**
Create at least one unique injury for the new damage type in [[2.3 Injuries]]. Unique injuries apply when a creature takes Health damage from that damage type and fails the injury save. They should be thematically appropriate: Fire causes burns, Ice/Cold causes frostbite, Necrotic causes necrosis, etc. Each injury needs an effect, a severity (how much it debilitates), and an end condition (time, healing, magic removal). Look at existing unique injuries for formatting. You may need to add a new condition for this, add it to [[Eyum TTRPG/2.0 Reference Tables/2.4 Conditions|2.4 Conditions]] if necessary and define all mechanics. 

**Deity (5.0 Deities)**
Assign or create a deity for the new affinity in [[5.0 Deities (WIP)]]. Every affinity should have at least one deity representing it, added to [[5.2 Specific Deities]]. A deity entry needs: name, appearance description, personality, domain (what they rule over, both physical plain and plane of existence), their role in the cosmos, how their followers worship them, and if they offer pacts (Godly/Fiendish/Ancient). If the deity already exists but isn't fleshed out, write their full entry. If the affinity is a sub-element (like Magma being under Fire/Earth), the deity should reflect the combined domain. If a deity already covers the concept (e.g., Darwin covers Earth), the new affinity's deity should be distinct.

**Slime (7.3.1.1 Slimes)**
Create a slime variant for the new affinity in [[7.3.1.1 Slimes]]. Every affinity gets one slime. A slime entry needs: its name, its affinity, its Weight Multiplier and Size Multiplier (determining how big and heavy it is relative to a standard slime), a brief description of its appearance and behavior, its stat modifiers (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA relative to baseline), any movement types beyond walking (swim, fly, burrow, climb), its damage type, its unique skills/passives/actives, and the drop it provides when killed (the jar of slime mass, which goes into the Slime Drops table in [[2.6.2 Animal and Monster Materials]]).

**Golem (7.3.1.2 Golems)**
Create a golem variant for the new affinity in [[7.3.1.2 Golems]]. Every affinity gets one golem. A golem entry needs: name, affinity, Appearance Modifier (how it looks different from the standard golem), stat modifier columns, a detailed physical description (paragraph of its appearance, materials, and behavior), movement types, its damage type, unique skills/passives/actives, its drops (the material harvested from it), and its Core Affinity modifiers (the golem's core is made of its element).

**Dragon (7.3.1.3 Dragons)**
Create a dragon variant for the new affinity in [[7.3.1.3 Dragons]]. Every affinity gets one dragon. A dragon entry needs: name, affinity, a detailed physical description, Weight Multiplier and Size Multiplier, stat modifiers, Damage Mod (the dice of extra elemental damage added to its attacks), unique active abilities beyond the generic dragon ones, and its harvestable materials: Dragon Scales (with full material stats: Damage, Accuracy, AC Bonus, Tool Modifier, Enchantability, Price Mod, Price/lbs), Dragon Meat, and Dragon Bones. The scales are the most mechanically detailed part: they define what damage type the weapon deals, what bonus dice are added, and what resistances/immunities the armor grants.

**Material Tables (2.6 Materials)**
Add all harvestable materials from the new slime, golem, and dragon to [[2.6 Materials]] and [[Eyum TTRPG/6.0 Magic/6.3 Alchemy|6.3 Alchemy]]:

- **Slime Drops:** Add a jar entry to the Slime Drops table in [[2.6.2 Animal and Monster Materials#Slime Drops]]. Needs name, source, usage (what it does when drunk/touched/thrown), and price.
- **Golem Drops:** Add the golem's harvested material to [[2.6.3 Special and Other Materials#Harvested]] with name, source, usage, and price/lbs.
- **Dragon Scales:** Add to the Scales/Shells/Scutes table in [[2.6.2 Animal and Monster Materials#Scales-Shells-Scutes]] with full material stats.
- **Dragon Meat:** Add to the Meat table in [[2.6.2 Animal and Monster Materials#Meat]] with description, taste, rations, and price/lbs.
- **Dragon Bones:** Add to the Bones table in [[2.6.2 Animal and Monster Materials#Bones]] with full material stats.
- **Dragon Eggs:** If applicable, add to the Eggs table in [[2.6.2 Animal and Monster Materials#Eggs]] with description, taste, incubation time/conditions, rations, weight, size, and price.

**Spells (6.1 Spells)**
Write spells for the new affinity in [[6.1.1 Elemental Spells]]:
- At least one **leveled spell** with full upcasting tiers. Each tier increases mana cost, damage, range, and effects. Follow the existing spell format: name, mana cost, range, affinity, description, upcast rule, and prerequisites.
- At least one **unique spell** (optional but recommended). Unique spells don't follow level tiers and can have creative non-combat effects. These go in [[6.1.2 Unique, Racial, and Healing Spells]].
- The leveled spell chain should cover the full power progression: a weak tier-1 version accessible with low affinity, scaling up to a powerful version requiring high affinity and level.

**Character Generators**
Update any tools that reference affinities:
- Update the Character Manager spell/gear generators if they reference specific affinity lists.
- Add the affinity to any dropdown menus, lookup tables, or validation code used by the character builder website or scripts.
