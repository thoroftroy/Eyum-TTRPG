## Tools/Equipment
Tools can be made from most materials listed in the Materials section. 

Proficiency and expertise with tools work as follows:
- **Proficiency:** You know how to use the tool for its intended purpose. When making a check the tool applies to, you add its material's Tool Modifier to the roll. Without proficiency, you do not add this modifier. The modifiers are listed in the material table in [[2.6.1 Metals and Stones Materials#Standard-Metals]].
- **Expertise:** You have Advantage on checks made with that tool.
