## Food
> Foods will last from the moment they are made to the duration of their Preservation column, for simplicity count their manufactured date as the purchased date unless otherwise specified. After the preservation date a food is considered Spoiled in which case it counts for half the ration amount and gives the Sickened condition for 1 day if eaten. If a food as a - in the preservation column it will never go bad. 
>
> You need to eat 2 meals/rations* a day or you will gain 1 level of exhaustion at the end of the day.
