Weapons, armors, and tools are the physical equipment your character uses for combat, defense, and skilled work. This section covers everything from the sword you swing and the armor that protects you to the rope you climb, the food you eat, and the consumables you throw. Your effectiveness with most equipment depends on whether you are trained in it: proficiency lets you use it without penalty, expertise makes you exceptionally skilled with it. The detailed rules for proficiency and expertise are covered in each item's specific file, since the mechanics differ between weapons, armor, shields, and tools.
### Currency
All prices in these tables are listed in the Eyum currency system. The base price assumes the item is made from the default material listed (usually Iron for metal goods, Wood for shields, or Leather for light armor). Items made from rarer materials multiply the base price by the material's Price modifier, found in [[2.6.1 Metals and Stones Materials]].

### How to Obtain Equipment
Most items can be purchased from shops in towns and cities: blacksmiths for weapons and metal tools, leatherworkers for armor and hide gear, general stores for supplies, goods stores and bakeries for consumables, and alchemist shops for consumables and poisons. You can also craft equipment yourself if you have the right materials, tools, and relevant skills (see [[Eyum TTRPG/6.0 Magic/6.3 Alchemy|6.3 Alchemy]]). Looting enemies is another source, though scavenged gear may be damaged. Some specialized items, like particular monster materials used for crafting, must be harvested from slain creatures (see [[2.6.2 Animal and Monster Materials]] for yields and pricing).
### Content Breakdown
#### [[2.5.1 Supplies and Services]]
Supplies are consumable items, single use goods, and everyday necessities that don't fall under weapons, armor, or tools. They are purchased with flat prices rather than material multipliers because the material they're made from doesn't significantly affect their function. The file is organized into these tables:

- **Food:** Edible rations and ingredients.
	- **Name**, the item's name.
	- **Usage**, mechanical effect, taste, special properties.
	- **# of Rations**, how many meals the item provides.
	- **Preservation**, how long before spoiling, "-" means never. Food spoils after its preservation period and grants the Sickened condition if eaten spoiled.
	- **Price**, how expensive the item is.
- **Drink:** Beverages from water to fine wine.
	- **Name**, the item's name.
	- **Usage**, volume, intoxication rules, mechanical effects.
	- **Hydration**, how much hydration that drink grants.
	- **Price**, how expensive the item is. 
- **Medical & Survival:** Healing and survival items.
	- **Name**, the item's name.
	- **Usage**, healing amounts, save DCs, number of uses per item.
	- **Price**, how expensive the item is.
- **Services:** Inns and hirelings.
	- **Name**, the service.
	- **Description**, what you get for your money.
	- **Price**, how expensive the service is.
#### [[2.5.2 Weapons]]
Weapons covers all melee and ranged combat instruments plus specialized ammunition and siege engines. The file opens with the weapon proficiency and expertise system and the full list of weapon type tags. The file contains these tables:

- **Weapons:** All personal melee and ranged weapons with their combat stats.
	- **Name**, the weapon's name.
	- **Base Damage**, dice and damage type.
	- **Base Range**, melee reach or ranged normal/max in feet.
	- **Type(s)**, the weapon's type tags, which determine proficiency requirements and combat rules.
	- **Description**, flavor and special mechanics.
	- **Price (Iron)**, the base price assuming Iron construction; multiply by a material's Price modifier from [[2.6.1 Metals and Stones Materials]] for other materials.

- **Arrows & Bolts:** Specialized ammunition with tracking rules. Mundane ammunition isn't tracked, but special material, enchanted, or specialty ammunition must be. After firing, roll 1d20: on 10+ the ammo is recoverable, on 9 or below it breaks. Ammunition enchanted with the Unbreakable Rune is always recoverable.
	- **Name**, the ammunition type.
	- **Damage**, flat damage added.
	- **Supported Weapons**, which weapons can fire it.
	- **Notes/Effects**, special keywords like Piercing, Crushing, Barbed, Hunter, and Blunt; each gains an additional bonus equal to half the material modifier.
	- **Price (Iron)**, the base price assuming Iron construction.

- **Siege Weaponry:** Massive crew-served engines of war divided into three tables: Bolt Throwers, Stone Throwers, and Specialized Siege. Siege weapons use Intelligence for attack rolls, must be mounted to fire, require a crew to load, and deal double damage to structures.
	- **Name**, the siege weapon.
	- **Base Damage**, dice and damage type (all include +10 flat bonus).
	- **Base Range**, normal/max range in feet.
	- **Type(s)**, always includes Siege, Two-Handed, and Loading where applicable.
	- **Crew**, number of crew members required to operate.
	- **Loading**, number of loading actions required between shots.
	- **Projectiles**, how many pieces of ammunition are consumed per shot.
	- **Ammo Types**, which ammunition from the Siege Ammunition table the weapon can fire.
	- **Min Range** (Stone Throwers only), shortest distance the weapon can target.
	- **Burst** (Stone Throwers only), radius of the impact area for DEX saves.
	- **Description**, special mechanics, structure multipliers, assembly times.
	- **Price (Iron)**, base price assuming Iron construction.
#### [[2.5.3 Armors]]
Armors covers protective gear and shields. The file contains these tables:

- **Armor:** All armor types with their defensive stats.
	- **Name**, the armor type.
	- **AC**, the base AC bonus added to your 10 base.
	- **Type**, Light, Medium, or Heavy; determines proficiency requirements and Max Dex Bonus caps.
	- **No Proficiency Penalty**, what happens if you wear the armor without proficiency (AC reduction, Stealth Disadvantage, speed penalties).
	- **Max Dex Bonus**, how much of your Dexterity modifier's AC conversion (from the table in [[1.3 Player Stats]]) you can apply while wearing this armor.
	- **Max Dex Bonus (Expert)**, the raised Dex cap if you have Expertise with this armor type.
	- **Notes**, what materials the armor can be made from and special crafting rules.
	- **Price (Iron)**, base price; multiply by material Price modifier for other materials.

- **Shields:** Defensive items held in one hand.
	- **Name**, the shield type.
	- **Base AC**, the shield's AC value.
	- **Description**, special mechanics like movement penalties, mounted bonuses, and deployable cover.
	- **Price (Wood)**, base price assuming Wood construction.
#### [[2.5.4 Tools and Equipment]]
Tools and Equipment covers everything for skilled work, adventuring comfort, mounts, and everyday carry items. The file contains these tables:

- **Tools/Equipment:** Items that can serve as improvised weapons, each listing their weapon stats alongside their tool function.
	- **Name**, the item's name.
	- **Description**, the tool's intended use and any skill check bonuses granted.
	- **Weapon To Hit**, accuracy modifier if used as an improvised weapon.
	- **Weapon Damage**, damage die and type if used as a weapon.
	- **Base Price (Iron)**, the base price assuming Iron construction.

- **Clothing:** Outfits for different climates and social situations.
	- **Name**, the item's name.
	- **Description**, mechanical bonuses to skill checks and environmental immunities.
	- **Price**, how expensive the item is.

- **Adventuring Gear:** Reusable utility items.
	- **Name**, the item's name.
	- **Usage**, mechanical function, weight limits, save DCs, special rules.
	- **Price**, how expensive the item is.

- **Kits & Packs:** Pre bundled equipment sets for quick character creation.
	- **Name**, the kit's name.
	- **Contents**, complete inventory of what's included.
	- **Price**, how expensive the kit is.

- **Animal Feed & Misc:** Mount feed, stabling, cages, saddlery accessories.
	- **Name**, the item's name.
	- **Usage**, what it does and how to use it.
	- **Price**, how expensive the item is.

- **Mounts/Familiar Equipment:** Saddles and armor for mounts and familiars.
	- **Name**, the equipment type.
	- **Description**, what type of mount it fits and any special rules.
	- **Price**, how expensive the item is.
