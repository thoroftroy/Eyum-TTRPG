- Commoners live in copper, adventurers/mercenaries live in gold and platinum. That gap is fine and intended (1 Platinum = 100,000 Copper; a hired mercenary makes ~14,000x a laborer's daily wage). Adventurers are walking fortunes and should feel like it.
- Sanity checks: cow 1g = 10,000c, a farmhand at 7c/day needs ~4 years to save for one (plausible pre-industrial). Longsword 3g = 30,000c = 10+ years of laborer income (historically a sword was months-to-years of common wages, fine). ## Buy/sell rules (currently missing, must be defined)

- Listed prices are shop purchase prices. The handbook never says what players get when SELLING, but 3.3 Leveling Up references "the buyer's resale value, as defined in 2.6 Materials" and 2.6 never defines it. Define it in the economy file.
- Proposals:
  - Default sell price: 50% of listed price.
  - "Buyer's resale value" = the listed price (merchants resell at list). The sell cap is 90% of that.
  - Trader feat (3.4): sell at +20% and buy at -10%, capped at 90% of buyer's resale value (consistent with the above).
  - Butcher-style skills (3.3): harvested materials sell at +10%, same 90% cap.
  - Alchemist L6: alchemical products sell at +50% (3.3) — check how this stacks with the cap.
- Enchanted items in shops: +50% markup on top of material and rune price mods (6.2 Runes, example in 1.6 Other Rules). Normal items have no markup, table price is the shop price. State this explicitly.
- Coin weight: Belt Pouch (2.5.4) holds ~100 coins or 1 lb -> 1 coin ≈ 0.16 oz ≈ 4.5 g. Canonize this in the economy file.
- Currency exchange/moneychangers: nothing exists. Optional to add (exchange fees, banks).

## Price anomalies found while auditing (fix later)

- **Salt (2.5.1): 1 Gold/lb.** Generic Meat is 50 Copper/lb, so salt costs 200x meat. Real salt is cheap (~0.2x meat). Also breaks salt-curing math (1g of salt to preserve 10 lbs of meat worth 5s total). Suggest 1-5 Silver/lb. Although salt would have been more expensive in a mediivil world it wouldnt be this much more expensive
- **Feast Meal 1g = 100 quality rations.** Real feast vs good meal is ~10x. Maybe fine since it fully restores Vitality + High Morale, flag for review.
- **Siege weapons (2.5.2):** the "Price (Iron)" column is in Nerite for stone throwers/special (Mangonel 2n, Trebuchet 10n, Hwacha 3n...) but Bolt Throwers are in Gold/Platinum (Scorpio 60g, Ballista 2p). Scorpio (60g) costs 3x a Ballista (2p = 20g) which is inverted. And applying material multipliers to a 10n trebuchet (x40 for Nerite = 400n) is absurd. Decide: flat siege prices, or the Nerite values are typos for Platinum.
- **Healer's Services 5g heals 1d8 HP** vs a Potency-1 Healing Potion at 50 Silver = 0.5g (6.3 Alchemy). Potions strictly better and 10x cheaper than the doctor. Either raise potion prices or drop the healer price.
- **Guide 5g/day vs Courier 1g between cities.** Fine but note guides are priced for adventurers.
- **Ostrich Egg 2s = 200 chicken eggs.** Real ostrich egg ≈ 80x a chicken egg. Close enough, fine.
- **Warhorse 5p vs Riding Horse 2g = 25x.** Real warhorse ≈ 20-50x. Fine.
- **Elephant 7p vs Lion 8p vs Tiger 10p (1.5).** Combat animals priced by danger, fine.
